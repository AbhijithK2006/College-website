Academic Departments & Curriculums

AIT offers standardized four-year bachelor's degrees and two-year postgraduate courses across engineering and computer science.

Academic Schools

Our degree courses are designed and administered by three core technical schools.

School of Computing

Offers undergraduate courses in Computer Science, Software Architecture, and Artificial Intelligence fields.

School of Engineering

Coordinates hands-on prototyping degrees in Robotics, Mechatronics, and Sustainable Electric Grids.

School of Tech Design

Focuses on Human-Computer Interaction, Interface Design paradigms, and virtual usability frameworks.

Campus Facilities

AIT supports academic learning through well-maintained spaces and specialized libraries.

  • Workstation Classrooms Dual-monitor desktop hubs integrated with testing database sandboxes.
  • Electronics Lab Equipped with oscilloscopes, signal boards, mechatronics kits, and soldering stations.
  • Main Library Quad Three floors of print volumes, journals, and dedicated research study desks.
  • Digital Libraries Every student receives online credentials to consult IEEE archives and cataloged journals 24/7.
  • Study Lounges Comfortable workrooms for students preparing projects, hackathons, and tech seminars.
