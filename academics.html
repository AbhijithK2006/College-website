State-of-the-Art

Academic Learning Infrastructure

AIT supports academic learning through well-maintained spaces, high-speed fiber connectivity, and dedicated research pods.

  • Workstation Classrooms Dual-monitor desktop hubs integrated with testing database sandboxes and cloud virtual machines.
  • Electronics & Embedded Lab Equipped with digital storage oscilloscopes, signal generators, FPGA boards, and solder rework stations.
  • Main Library Quad Three floors of print volumes, academic journals, and dedicated faculty-student consultation cubicles.
  • Digital Journal Repositories Every student receives online credentials to consult IEEE, ACM, Springer, and ScienceDirect archives 24/7.
  • Innovation & Prototyping Sandbox Round-the-clock lab space for students working on startup ideas, capstone projects, and hackathons.
